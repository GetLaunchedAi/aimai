About Us

About AIMAI

AIMAI Auto Electronic Solutions is a trusted mobile installation business serving drivers, fleets, and equipment owners across Michigan. Since 2022, AIMAI has provided reliable electronic solutions for thousands of vehicles and heavy machinery—from personal cars and commercial fleets to generators and agricultural equipment.

Backed by professional certifications and years of field experience, AIMAI delivers intelligent, dependable, and innovative installations. Our mission is simple: engineer trust, restore confidence, and provide electronic solutions for anything powered by a battery and motor.

AIMAI Auto Electronic Solutions

At AIMAI Auto Electronic Solutions, we specialize in professional mobile electronics installation that enhances your vehicle’s performance, security, and connectivity. From GPS tracking systems and 360-degree camera setups to remote start installations, audio amplifiers, and custom telematics solutions, our team designs and installs systems that are tailored to your exact needs.

With over a decade of hands-on experience, we bring technical precision and a deep understanding of vehicle electronics to every project. Whether you're upgrading a personal car, equipping a fleet of trucks, or optimizing heavy machinery, we provide durable, high-performance installations that improve safety, convenience, and peace of mind.

Our services cover cars, boats, trucks, RVs, and other battery-powered or motor-driven equipment. Every installation is executed with careful calibration to ensure peak functionality in real-world conditions. From keyless entry systems to kill switch integration, AIMAI delivers cutting-edge auto electronic solutions that stand the test of time.

Boost the value, functionality, and security of your vehicles—trust AIMAI Auto Electronic Solutions to bring your vision to life with expert service and innovative technology.
